Part 2: Privacy for App and Social Media

1. Which data is collected when using the app?
When you download and use my app, I, together with the third-party providers integrated into it (for example for advertising or error analysis), automatically collect certain technical information. This is technically necessary in order to provide the game functions, save game progress, fix errors, prevent misuse, and, where applicable, display advertising.

The following data and identifiers are collected:

Device and metadata: IP address, device type or device model (for example iPhone 14 or Samsung Galaxy), operating system used and its version (iOS / Android), the system language selected, and your device's time zone.

Advertising identifiers (mobile advertising ID): I process device-specific advertising identifiers. On Apple devices this is the “Identifier for Advertisers” (IDFA), on Android devices the “Google Advertising ID” (AAID). These identifiers are used to display personalized advertising (after your explicit consent), limit the frequency of certain ads, and measure the success of advertising campaigns.

Specific device identifiers (Device ID): Unique device-related identifiers such as the Android ID or the “Identifier for Vendor” (IDFV) on Apple devices. These are used for internal analysis, the detection of fraud or multiple accounts, and the stability of the app.

Interaction and game data: Information about your usage behavior within the app. This includes access timestamps, session duration, used in-app features, in-app purchases, and error and crash reports (crash logs).

Platform accounts (optional): If my app offers sign-in via Apple Game Center or Google Play Games and you use it, I receive a system-specific, pseudonymized user ID from these platforms. This serves solely to securely store your game progress in the cloud and synchronize it across devices.

Legal basis:
The processing of device and game data for the provision of the app and to ensure its functionality is based on Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR (performance of a contract). The use of advertising identifiers for tracking and personalized advertising is carried out exclusively on the basis of your consent pursuant to Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a GDPR.

2. Objection and deactivation of advertising IDs (opt-out)
You can restrict, block, or reset the collection and use of your mobile advertising ID for personalized advertising at any time in your smartphone's system settings:

Apple (iOS): Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and deactivate the switch for “Allow Apps to Request to Track”. You can also specifically disable tracking for my app.

Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and select “Delete advertising ID” or (depending on the Android version) enable “Opt out of Ads Personalization”.

As soon as you deactivate the toggle, no further analytics data will be transmitted from your device to me or to third-party providers. The lawfulness of the data processing that took place before your withdrawal remains unaffected.
